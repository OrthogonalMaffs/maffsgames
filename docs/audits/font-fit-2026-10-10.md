# FONT-FIT findings: the phone-fit checks in the fonts students see (10 Oct 2026)

Contract FONT-FIT (`docs/handover/contracts/2026-10-10-font-fit.md`), cloud lane. Every phone-fit check was re-run
twice from one checkout: once in the old way, with Google Fonts blocked and the runner's fallback font pinned to
DejaVu as CI pins it, and once in the real fonts, served from `scripts/fonts/`. KaTeX was real in both runs: CI never
blocked it. Both runs were seeded, so each game drew the same first question in each. Two real-font runs of the fold
report differ in 1 cell of about 580, so the differences below come from the fonts, not from the draws.

## What the contract assumed, and what was measured

- **KaTeX was never the fallback.** CI's checks load KaTeX from the CDN, so its glyphs were already measured. Only
  Google's faces fell back: Outfit, JetBrains Mono and five more on a few pages.
- **Simultaneous Solver's "keypad 25 px below the fold" did not reproduce.** In real and in fallback fonts the
  keypad fitted on Stages 1 to 4. On a true first visit (fonts held back until the step had rendered and scrolled,
  then released), the step moved down 8-9 px when they landed and still ended 20 px above the fold at 320×568.
  Its real faults were elsewhere (below).

## Simultaneous Solver

- **Stages 1 to 4: fixed in the page.**
  - **The fault:** `stepInView()` aimed for an 8 px margin, but it only scrolled once the controls were *past* the
    fold. A step ending 1-7 px above the fold stayed there: 6.9 px at 320×568, and 7.6-7.8 px elsewhere.
  - **The fix:** one in-view rule, `fitInView`, for the step, Next and Stage 5's form. It scrolls in whole pixels and
    is fitted again once the fonts arrive.
  - **Measured after the fix,** six problems a stage, every step, Check and Next: at least **8.0 px** at 320×568,
    375×667 and 390×844, in both the real and the fallback fonts.
- **Stage 5 (the word problems): cannot fit at 320×568 without a cut.**
  - **The size of it:** 15 of the 40 problems fail, even with the problem's words scrolled to the top. They are 21
    to 53 px over with both lines built:
    - W04, W08, W17, W18, E10, E13 and E18: 53 px
    - W02, W03 and E02: 28 px
    - E03, E06, E14 and E15: 23 px
    - E09: 21 px
  - **Why:** the room between the margins is 512 px. W04 needs 565 px:
    - the words: 74 px
    - "Let …": 57 px
    - the instruction "Write the two equations. Tap a line, then tap tiles to build it.", two lines: 64 px
    - the lines and the tiles: 311 px
    - Check: 44 px
  - **The shortest version that fits:** the instruction shown on the first problem only. That saves 64 px, so all 40
    fit, W04 with 11 px to spare. This is a content cut, so it is Jon's call (STOP IF).
  - At 375×667 and 390×844 every problem fits.
- **The self-test, as the contract words it, cannot be made.** Real against fallback fonts moves Simultaneous
  Solver's Stages 1 to 4 by about 1 px, so no plant of the old font-blocking can make its 320×568 assertion fail.
  Pages where the fonts do decide the verdict:
  - `distinctly-average`'s width (+14 px in the real fonts, fits in the fallback);
  - `standard-form-blitz`'s Next (10 px over in the real fonts);
  - Simultaneous Solver's own Stage 5 at 375×667 (fits by 10.9 px in the real fonts, 9.9 px over in the fallback).
