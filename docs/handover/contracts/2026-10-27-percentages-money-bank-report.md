# PERCENTAGES-MONEY-BANK: the report (27 Oct 2026)

**PERCENTAGES-MONEY-BANK (27 Oct, Jon's contract; PR and main run: see the line in `home.md` once merged).** A `percentages-money` bank of **60 generated questions**, 12 in each of five stages, as the bank-first source for the future game "It's on Sale!" (canon 8.2.1). Setting: a high-street shop through the year (summer sale, Black Friday, January sales, price rises), a savings account, a car dealer. Plain wording, no jokes.

**Stages:** 1 percentage of an amount (money: how much is taken off); 2 increase or decrease (money: the new price; 7 off, 5 on); 3 percentage change (number with % beside the box; 7 decreases, 5 increases); 4 reverse percentages (money: the price before; 8 reductions, 4 rises); 5 interest and depreciation (money; **3 simple** (25%), 5 compound, 4 depreciation, 2 to 5 years' worth of working). No multiple choice, no new answer format.

**Method and calculator:** stages 1-4 are `calc: "optional"`, worked from 10%, 5% or 1% ("£60 is 80%, so 1% is 75p, 100% is £75"); the amount is always a whole number of pounds, so every step is whole pence. **"Try without a calculator" on 38 of the 48** (every amount in the working a multiple of 5p, every multiplier or divisor 12 or under or a multiple of ten; the rule is recomputed in the verifier). Stage 5 is `calc: true`, worked with the multiplier, year by year, then as a power, names whether simple or compound interest is asked, and says "to the nearest penny" (the working shows the unrounded value, so rounding happens once). No answer is a half-penny.

**Slips (matched by value, each only where it is a distinct, exact answer):** stage 2 the percentage given as the price; stage 3 the change divided by the new price (offered in 2 of 12: elsewhere the value is not one a student can type exactly); stage 4 the percentage taken off the sale price (offered where it is whole pence); stage 5 compound worked as simple and the reverse, rate times years, the digit of the rate as the multiplier (1.2 for a 2% rise, 0.2 for 20% off), depreciation added instead of taken off.

**Checks:** `pm_expected()`/`pm_faults()` in `gen-six-ps.py` recompute every answer and slip from the question's own params with exact fractions (never the generator's integer code), the whole-pence working, the friendly flag, the half-penny test and the words of each stem; `pm_selftest()` plants ten faults, each caught. `gen-six-ps.py --check` passes on all 34 banks; `gen-exam-topics.py --check` passes. Topic row: `percentages-money`, 13.3 marks a series (the old percentage-change 6.0 + interest-depreciation 7.3), 6 series, bands 3 to 5.
