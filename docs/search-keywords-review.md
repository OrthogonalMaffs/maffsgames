# Search keywords: review table (contract SEARCH-KEYWORDS, 4 Nov 2026)

For Project Claude's review of the content before merge. The keywords live in `data/games.json` (`keywords` on each game, and on The 6 P's `pages` record); `scripts/apply-meta.py` writes them onto every card as `data-keywords`, and `scripts/check-meta.py` holds both in CI. They are for on-site search only (`schools/assets/search.js`), never shown and never SEO.

**How they were made.** Each game's keywords were drafted from its own page and bank (stage names, question types, worked examples, the generator or bank file it loads) and its spec-map rows, then checked here: the format rules, and spot greps of the riskiest claims against the bank (factorials in Higher Power, phasors in Complex Converter, Cramer's rule in Matrix Crunch, the topics in I Found a Mark's templates and others). A spec row the bank does not teach was never used (listed at the end). *Evidence* is the drafting note on where in the game each topic comes from.

**Decided for you (list for Jon):**
- "year 6" and "ks2" are not used: they are not among the contract's level words, and SR-11 keeps Year 6 out of every title and description.
- "resit" is on the Resit Fluency Strand games (and I Found a Mark and Who Ate All the Pies?, both in that strand), on The 6 P's (made for the November resits), and on **Linear Equation Solver**, which is GCSE Foundation fluency but not in the strand: drop it there if "resit" should mean the strand only.
- Estimation Golf: "unit conversion" dropped (no conversion question found in its bank).
- The 6 P's is a page, not a game: its keywords sit on its existing `pages` record and describe what it always is (Foundation revision, mixed topics), not its 35 possible topics, since any one session draws only six.
