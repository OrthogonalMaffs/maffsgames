How it works

A number is hidden. You get one clue about it to start: guess whenever you like, or ask for the next clue. Every clue is true of the number, and after the last clue only one number fits. Six guesses a number.

A wrong guess tells you Higher or Lower, how close you are, and which of your clues it breaks. The closeness words, nearest first: Incredibly close, Very warm, Getting warmer, In the right area, Far off. They scale with the range: within 1 of 100 is as close as within 10 of 1000. The colour of the closeness word shows how near you are, not whether to go higher or lower.

“Between 41 and 50” includes 41 and 50. Each number is worth 100, less 20 for each clue after the first and 10 for each wrong guess (at least 10 if you find it).
