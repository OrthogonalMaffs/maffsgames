⚓ Bearing Blitz

You are a submarine. Enemy ships are closing in.
Set your bearing by eye — no numbers, no labels. Fire or die.
Drag the needle to the target bearing. You have no readout — judge it by eye using the tick marks.
±5° tolerance = hit. Outside = miss + depth charge.
Three lives. Lose them all and the surface ships destroy you.

Remember: bearings are measured clockwise from the top (North).
