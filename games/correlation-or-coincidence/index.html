Correlation or Coincidence?

Two things rise and fall together. Why?
GCSE

Each question is a scatter graph of two things, both named on the graph. They are correlated: as one goes up, the other tends to go up (or down). Decide why.

Three possible answers

  • One causes the other. Change one and the other changes because of it.
  • Something else causes both. A third thing, the lurking variable, drives them both.
  • Coincidence. Nothing links them: the pattern turned up by chance.
