Just Pythag It, Bruv

Pythagoras' theorem. Find the hypotenuse, or find a shorter side.

Foundation Calculator required
a² + b² = c²

Every question is a right-angled triangle. Start by finding the hypotenuse: the longest side, opposite the right angle.

Finding the hypotenuse? Square the other two sides and add. Is the hypotenuse given? Then you're finding a shorter side: subtract. Then square root.

Three rounds, in order

  • 1Whole-number triangles. Some you might spot without a calculator.
  • 2Decimal answers, to 1 decimal place.
  • 3Real situations: ladders, screens, ramps, fields and roofs.
