MaffsCards

A compare-and-win number card game. You against Marvin, the computer, who is studying humans.

Every card is a number with six values: sum of factors, largest prime factor, Roman numeral length, distance to the nearest square, superpowers and how often it turns up in pi. Pick a value you think beats Marvin's card. Highest wins, except distance to a square: lowest wins.

Win the round and you take both cards, and you choose next. A tie sends both cards to the middle pile, and the next round's winner takes those too.
