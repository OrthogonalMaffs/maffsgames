Possibly, Maybe

Probability, from the scale to tree diagrams.

Probability is a number from 0 (it cannot happen) to 1 (it must happen). Each stage starts with a worked example, then ten questions, easiest first.

Type fractions in the two boxes, top and bottom: 2/4 counts the same as 1/2 unless the question asks for the simplest form. Decimals and whole numbers go in the box. No calculator needed.
