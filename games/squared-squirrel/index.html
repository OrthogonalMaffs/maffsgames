Football Pitches per Squared Squirrel

Read the unit, and you know the calculation.

Per means for each, and it means divide: km/h is km ÷ h, and £/kg is £ ÷ kg.

So football pitches per squared squirrel is football pitches ÷ (squirrel × squirrel). The unit tells you what to do.
