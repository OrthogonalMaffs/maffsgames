MISSION BRIEFING

read once · then fire at will
Objective

Destroy all three enemy tanks. You and your opponent fire in turn.

Controls

Set ANGLE (θ, 5°–85°) and POWER (V, 20–100), then FIRE.

The trigonometry

Your shell leaves the barrel at speed V, angle θ. That one velocity splits into two independent parts:

Vx = V · cos(θ)  →  carries it across
Vy = V · sin(θ)  →  carries it up, then gravity takes it back down

Raise θ and you trade distance for height. 45° gives maximum range — that is the angle where sin and cos are equal, so the shell gets the best possible split between across and up.

Live Trig Breakdown

The centre panel recalculates as you move the sliders, before you commit. Read Vx and Vy off it and you can reason about where the shell will land instead of guessing.

Ranging in

Your first shot is not supposed to hit. Watch whether it falls short or long, adjust, and fire again. Bracketing the target over two or three shots is how real artillery works — a miss is information, not a mistake.
