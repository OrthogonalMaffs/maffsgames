Who Ate All the Pies?

Pie charts, from the stall on matchday.

A pie chart shows how a whole is shared out. The whole chart is 360°, so a slice's angle over 360 is its fraction of the pies.

Each stage starts with a worked example, then ten questions. Type your answers in the boxes. A calculator is optional, behind the Calculator button: some questions say to try it with pen and paper.
