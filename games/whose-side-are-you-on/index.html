Whose Side Are You On?

Label the sides of a right-angled triangle from the marked angle, then link them to sin, cos and tan.

Every side gets its name from the marked angle. Each side keeps its colour, its line and its letter all the way through.

HHYP is the longest, across from the right angle. OOPP is across from the marked angle. AADJ touches the marked angle and is not the longest.

Ten quick questions a stage. No lives: a faster right answer scores more, a slow one is still right.
