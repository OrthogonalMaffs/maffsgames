Y So Steep?

The equation of a straight line, one step at a time.

Every straight line is y = mx + c. m says how steep it is, and c says where it crosses the y-axis. Each round is six questions. Type m and c into the two boxes.

The longer questions are split into steps: the gradient, then c, then the whole equation. Each step is its own mark.
