Why OP for Secondary?

The biggest predictor of a student struggling in Year 7 maths isn't which primary school they attended — it's a small number of specific gaps arriving from Year 6.

These games target exactly those gaps: negative numbers, algebraic thinking, formula substitution, and decimal fluency. Every game is free, requires no sign-up, and is safe for school networks.
