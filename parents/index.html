Help Your Child With Maths

You don't need to be a maths teacher. You just need to understand what they're learning.

Maths has changed since you were at school. The methods are different, the language is different, and the curriculum has expanded. That's not your fault — and it doesn't mean you can't help.

These guides explain each topic in plain English, show you exactly how schools teach it now, and link to free practice games your child can use. No sign-up. No cost. Just clear explanations from a working maths tutor.

Common Questions

Why is maths taught differently now?

Methods have evolved to build deeper understanding. Grid multiplication and bar models are steps on the way to the written methods you learned, rather than replacements for them; long division is still the formal written method the National Curriculum names for Year 6. The answers are the same — the routes are different.

Do I need to be good at maths to help?

No. These guides assume you've forgotten everything. They start from scratch and explain in plain English.

What if I teach them the wrong method?

Show them you're interested, not that you know the method. These guides show the method schools use now so you can support rather than confuse.

How much should I help with homework?

Guide, don't do. Ask "what do you think the first step is?" rather than showing them the answer. The struggle is where learning happens.

What do "Higher tier" and "Foundation tier" mean?

GCSE maths has two tiers. Foundation tier is graded 1 to 5. Higher tier is graded 4 to 9, and a grade 3 is allowed on the Higher tier.
