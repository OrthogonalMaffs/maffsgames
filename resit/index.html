GCSE Resit

When your teaching mentor from your first teaching job asks you for resources for a GCSE resit class, you jump to it. That's why this section exists: to support students who are working hard for that magical grade 4, and the many doors it opens. If you'd like another game added, just drop us a line. We're here to help.

Each card says what the game practises, its references on our spec map, and which level to choose for Foundation content.

Number

Six Sevens, Bruv Times tables to 12 × 12, typed answers, with a grid that lights up as each fact is learned. GCSE spec: N2 One level Free Daily Pizza Converting between fractions, decimals and percentages, with a new round every day. GCSE spec: N10–N11, N12, R9, R3 One level Negative Number Line Placing, ordering and calculating with negative numbers on a number line. GCSE spec: N2, N1 One level Decimal Detective Ordering, rounding and placing decimals on a number line. GCSE spec: N14–N16, N1 One level Think of a Number “I think of a number” puzzles, solved by undoing each step. GCSE spec: N3 One level Factor Race Two numbers: pick the one with more factors. GCSE spec: N4 Opens at: Starter Prime Sprint Building a number’s prime factorisation, one prime at a time. GCSE spec: N4 Opens at: Starter Estimation Engine Rounding each number to 1 significant figure, then estimating: a mark for each step. GCSE spec: N14–N16 One level Estimation Golf Estimating answers: the closer the estimate, the nearer the hole. GCSE spec: N14–N16 Choose: Foundation Unit Converter Converting units of length, area and volume. GCSE spec: N13 Choose: GCSE

Algebra

Formula Plug-In Substituting values into formulae. GCSE spec: R11, A2, G16 One level Like Terms Collector Collecting like terms, building up to full simplification. GCSE spec: A4 One level Linear Equation Solver Solving one- and two-step equations by choosing the inverse operation at each step. GCSE spec: A17 Choose: GCSE Equation Builder Turning a word problem into an equation or formula. GCSE spec: A21 Choose: Foundation Four Quadrant Explorer Plotting and reading coordinates in all four quadrants. GCSE spec: A8 One level Formula Unlocked Changing the subject of a formula, with hints when needed. GCSE spec: A5 Choose: GCSE Formula Forge Rearranging formulae, step by step. GCSE spec: A5 Choose: GCSE Sequence Solver Next terms, term-to-term rules and the nth term. GCSE spec: A23–A25 Choose: Foundation

Ratio, proportion and percentages

Split It Ratio: simplifying, sharing, scaling recipes and unit pricing. Pick a mode first. GCSE spec: R9 Choose: Foundation Proportion Blaster Direct and inverse proportion, and sharing in a ratio. GCSE spec: R9 Choose: GCSE Better Value Comparing deals to find the better value. GCSE spec: R11 Choose: GCSE Percentage Flip Percentages of amounts, worked out mentally. GCSE spec: N10–N11, N12 Opens at: Starter

Geometry and measures

Geometry is the thinnest section for now. More games are on the way before the June 2027 exams.

New Shapes Area of parallelograms and trapezia, and volume of prisms. GCSE spec: G16 One level Angle Ace Finding missing angles and giving the reason. GCSE spec: G1–G3 Choose: Starter Shape Shifter Translating, reflecting and rotating shapes on a coordinate grid. Pick a mode first. GCSE spec: G7 One level

Probability

Probability Pioneer The probability scale, simple probabilities, and true-or-false statements. GCSE spec: P3–P4, P7 One level Expected Damage Using probabilities to work out expected outcomes and pick the better option. GCSE spec: P2 Opens at: Foundation Given That Probabilities from two-way tables, Venn diagrams and frequency trees. GCSE spec: P6, P9 Choose: GCSE

Statistics

Distinctly Average Mean, median, mode and range. GCSE spec: S1–S4 Choose: Foundation Stat Attack Grouped frequency tables: modal class, median class and estimated mean. GCSE spec: S1–S4 Choose: GCSE Chart Interrogator Reading and comparing statistical diagrams. Pick a diagram type too. GCSE spec: S1–S4 Choose: GCSE Correlation or Coincidence? Correlation and causation: does one cause the other, does something else cause both, or is it chance? GCSE spec: S6 One level
