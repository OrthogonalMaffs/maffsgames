Six short bursts on the topics that carry the most marks at GCSE Foundation, five questions each, in about 20 to 25 minutes. Each burst opens in one of our games and brings you back here when you've done.

Built closest to OCR's Foundation papers. These are the core skills every board's Foundation papers test, so it's useful whichever board you sit.

What it isn't

It isn't a mock or a predicted paper. For full past papers and mocks, these are the places to go:

  • Hannah Kettle Maths
  • Corbettmaths
  • Maths Genie
  • Mathslinks

Nothing here is timed against you, and nothing is a test. Get some wrong: that's where the marks are hiding. This is new and still being built, so tell us what works and what doesn't on the feedback page.
